CAMPUSCONNECT

Your campus. Your community. One place.

CampusConnect is a centralized hub for college student activities, bringing events, clubs, volunteering opportunities and campus announcements together in one organized place.

From officially authorized college events to student-led activities, CampusConnect makes it easier for students to discover what's happening around their campus, save events they're interested in, RSVP, connect with organizers and keep track of their activities.

What is CampusConnect?

College life generates a constant stream of events, opportunities and announcements. CampusConnect brings them together so students don't have to hunt through multiple notice boards, WhatsApp groups, Instagram pages, club messages, and class groups to find what's happening.

What can students do?

Discover

Find upcoming college events, club activities, volunteering opportunities and announcements.

Save

Bookmark events you're interested in so you can find them later.

RSVP

Reserve your place at events and keep track of events you've joined.

Stay Organized

Use My Events to see your RSVPs, saved events and upcoming activities.

Connect

Contact event organizers when you need additional information.

Explore

Filter and search events using categories, tags, dates and event formats.

Participate

Discover both official college activities and student-led initiatives.

Create

Students and organizations can publish events and opportunities for the campus community.

One Hub for Every Side of Campus Life

CampusConnect supports both official and unofficial activities:

Official College Events:Events authorized by the college or relevant college authorities.
Student Events:Unofficial/student-led activities organized by students or student groups.
Note: Event organizers are responsible for accurately declaring whether an event is officially authorized by the college. CampusConnect provides the distinction as an organizational feature and does not independently verify authorization.

Why CampusConnect?

CampusConnect aims to make campus life:

  • More organized
  • Easier to discover
  • Easier to participate in
  • Less dependent on scattered communication channels

Built With Security in Mind

  • Passwords are not stored as plaintext.
  • Authentication uses browser-based cryptographic APIs.
  • Users can only modify their own posts.
  • Organizer PINs provide an additional verification step for post management.
  • User-generated text is sanitized before being rendered.
  • External links are validated.
  • Sensitive organizer contact information is only displayed according to the organizer's chosen visibility setting.
Note: CampusConnect is a frontend-only academic project. A production deployment would require server-side authentication, secure server-side authorization, a database and protected backend services.
